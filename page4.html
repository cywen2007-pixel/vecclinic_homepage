Vertinary Service

At VEC Clinic in Petaling Jaya, we provide compassionate, full-spectrum veterinary care to keep your cats and dogs healthy at every life stage. Our cat-friendly clinic combines expert medicine with a stress-free environment, ensuring your pet receives the best possible care—whether for routine visits or complex condition
Vaccination

Protect your pet against common and potentially deadly diseases through scheduled core and optional vaccinations.

Parasite Prevention

Protect your pet from harmful parasites like fleas, ticks, ear mites, and intestinal worms that can cause serious illness—stay consistent with vet-recommended preventive treatments.

Laser Therapy

Regular dental check-ups and professional scaling to prevent tartar buildup, bad breath, and gum disease.
