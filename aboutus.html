A Wide Range of Services

We offer a broad spectrum of diagnostic procedures through in-house testing and the use of external laboratories. Our clinic includes a well-stocked pharmacy, well-equipped surgery suite, in-house digital X-ray capabilities, ultrasound and endoscopy services. We are also ready to be of service as a referral centre, should you and your pet need one.

At VEC Veterinary Clinic, our job is not only to treat your pet when he or she isn’t feeling well, but also to help you learn how to keep your best friend happy and healthy.

Our doors are open 7 days a week, making sure we’ll always be there for you, and your pet.

OUR MISSION

Our core mission is to help pets live longer, healthier and happier lives through preventive care. We believe in highlighting the importance of regular exams, even if your pet seems healthy. In the event of unfortunate circumstances, we are well equipped to provide exceptional emergency and critical care services. With our expertise and state-of-the-art equipments, each and every pet will get the treatment and care it deserves, in a comfortable and supportive environment.

OUR VISION

With great emphasis on fostering the growth and development of our veterinarians, our goal is to always provide expert medical care with empathy and integrity. All of our caring and passionate doctors share a single vision when it comes to veterinary care, which is to humanise healthcare for animals. Regardless of the situation, we’ll always be there for you, and your pet.
